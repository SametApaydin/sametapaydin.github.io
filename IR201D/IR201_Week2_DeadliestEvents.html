    Read with care

    • Death tolls are Matthew White's estimates. Many are contested, and for older events the range of scholarly estimates is very wide.
    • Years for 86 events were read from the positions on the Times graphic (about plus or minus 5 years). Only the 14 events in the graphic's own tables have exact years. These are not White's own dates.
    • Twenty events lasted less than about 8 years, too short to read from the image. Their deaths per year is an upper bound, so they are drawn hollow in that view and left out of the top 10.
    • World population before 1800 is a rough reconstruction (HYDE, via Our World in Data). Our shares are computed at each event's midpoint and differ from the Times figures, which used White's own population estimates (Genghis Khan: our 9.0% against the Times' 11.1%).
    • A share of humanity spread over a century is not comparable with one taken in a few years. Like the Times, we leave events longer than 40 years out of the share ranking and draw them hollow.
    • Deaths per year uses inclusive years (1939 to 1945 counts as 7 years), which reproduces the Times' own per-year table.
