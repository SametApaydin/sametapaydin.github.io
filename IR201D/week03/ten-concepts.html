Ten concepts, ten pictures

In May 2014 Stephen Walt offered a "Five Minute University" in international relations: five concepts a graduate still remembers five years later. Two days later Laura Sjoberg answered with five concepts he left out. Each concept below gets one picture and one look at the same event, the COVID-19 pandemic. Then pick your own five.

Readings

  • Walt, Stephen M. 2014. "How to Get a B.A. in International Relations in 5 Minutes." Foreign Policy, May 19, 2014.
  • Sjoberg, Laura. 2014. "'Mansplaining' International Relations? What Walt Misses." Relations International, May 21, 2014.

Data

  • Vetoes: United Nations, Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs, "Security Council Data: Vetoes since 1946" (psdata.un.org), downloaded October 12, 2026. Counts are vetoed drafts per year, split by the member that cast each veto. A draft vetoed by two members counts once for each.
  • Balance of power: the Week 2 data (World Bank GDP at market exchange rates, SIPRI military spending).
  • Trade, remittances, foreign investment, aid, life expectancy, under-five deaths, population: World Bank, World Development Indicators (databank.worldbank.org), CC BY 4.0. Series NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS, BX.TRF.PWKR.CD.DT, BX.KLT.DINV.CD.WD, DT.ODA.ALLD.CD, SP.DYN.LE00.IN, SH.DTH.MORT, SP.POP.TOTL.
  • Poverty: World Bank, Poverty and Inequality Platform (pip.worldbank.org), world headcount at $3.00, $4.20, $8.30 and $10 a day in 2021 prices (PPP). 2025 onward are nowcasts and are left out.
  • World Bank voting power: IBRD, "Subscriptions and Voting Power of Member Countries", data as of June 30, 2026.
  • Same-sex marriage: Equaldex, via Our World in Data (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0. Counts countries coded "Legal" nationwide.
  • CO₂ per person: Global Carbon Budget, via Our World in Data (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0.
  • COVID-19 vaccination: Our World in Data and WHO (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0. Share with at least one dose.
  • Iraq: US National Intelligence Estimate, Key Judgments (declassified excerpt, October 2002); Iraq Survey Group, Comprehensive Report of the Special Advisor to the DCI on Iraq's WMD (the Duelfer report), September 30, 2004.

Read with care

  • Where a concept sits on the zoom ladder, and which concepts explain which issue in "Your five", are our coding for class discussion. Argue with them.
  • Sjoberg's poverty numbers are from 2014. Price bases change: the same $10 line counts more people in older prices.
  • Remittances count money sent through formal channels. Aid data for this country group end in 2021.
