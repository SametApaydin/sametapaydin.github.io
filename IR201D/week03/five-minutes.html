What US intelligence said · October 2002

"Baghdad has chemical and biological weapons as well as missiles with ranges in excess of UN restrictions; if left unchecked, it probably will have a nuclear weapon during this decade."

US National Intelligence Estimate, Key Judgments

What inspectors found · September 2004

"ISG judges that Iraq unilaterally destroyed its undeclared chemical weapons stockpile in 1991. There are no credible indications that Baghdad resumed production of chemical munitions thereafter."

Iraq Survey Group (Duelfer) report

And on the other side

The same report found that Saddam Hussein believed doubt about his weapons helped deter his enemies. Misperception ran both ways.

Your five

If you could keep only five of these ten concepts, which would they be?

Walt’s five explain how states deal with each other. Sjoberg’s five ask who lives with the results, and where. Neither author says five is enough: Walt lists what a real expert would add, and Sjoberg calls her five a priority, not a complete list.

Data

  • Vetoes: United Nations, Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs, "Security Council Data: Vetoes since 1946" (psdata.un.org), downloaded October 12, 2026. A draft vetoed by two members counts once for each.
  • GDP shares: World Bank, World Development Indicators, GDP at market exchange rates. Military spending: SIPRI Military Expenditure Database. Both as used on the Week 2 page "One superpower?".
  • Trade, remittances, foreign investment, aid, life expectancy, under-five deaths, population: World Bank, World Development Indicators (databank.worldbank.org), CC BY 4.0. Series NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS, BX.TRF.PWKR.CD.DT, BX.KLT.DINV.CD.WD, DT.ODA.ALLD.CD, SP.DYN.LE00.IN, SH.DTH.MORT, SP.POP.TOTL.
  • Iraq: US National Intelligence Estimate, Key Judgments (declassified excerpt, October 2002); Iraq Survey Group, Comprehensive Report of the Special Advisor to the DCI on Iraq’s WMD (the Duelfer report), September 30, 2004.
  • Same-sex marriage: Equaldex, via Our World in Data (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0. Places coded "Legal" nationwide, including territories.
  • Poverty: World Bank, Poverty and Inequality Platform (pip.worldbank.org), world headcount at $3.00, $4.20, $8.30 and $10 a day in 2021 prices (PPP). 2025 onward are nowcasts and are left out.
  • World Bank votes: IBRD, "Subscriptions and Voting Power of Member Countries", data as of June 30, 2026.
  • CO₂ per person: Global Carbon Budget, via Our World in Data (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0.
  • COVID-19 vaccination: Our World in Data and WHO (ourworldindata.org), CC BY 4.0. Share with at least one dose.
