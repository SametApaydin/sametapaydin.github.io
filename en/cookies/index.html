This site uses no third-party cookies and loads no analytics tool. It keeps only the records below in your browser: the theme and cookie records are written when you click, the language record when you open a dashboard or switch language.

Name Purpose Duration Party Legal basis
trr-theme Remember the light or dark theme you chose Until you change it First party A service you explicitly requested (KVKK cookie guide, criterion B)
trr-lang Carry the language you chose into the dashboards Until you change it First party A service you explicitly requested (criterion B)
mra-consent Remember your choice in the cookie panel and not show it again 365 days First party Necessary (criterion B)

An analytics category appears in the panel, but no tool is loaded in this prototype; only your choice is recorded.

Changing your choice

The “Cookie settings” link at the foot of the page, or in the menu on dashboards, reopens the panel. Clearing your browser’s site data also removes all records.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights under article 11 of Law 6698 by applying to the controller above. See the Privacy page for how.
