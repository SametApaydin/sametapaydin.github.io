Privacy and personal data

This is a draft notice under article 10 of Turkish Law 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data; IPC’s legal office will review it before release. For visitors in the European Union the same information serves as the notice under articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR.

Controller

Istanbul Policy Center, Sabancı University; Bankalar Cad. No: 2 Minerva Han, 34420 Karaköy, Istanbul, Türkiye.

Which data, for what

  • Contact form: your name, e-mail address and message, to answer you.
  • Site preferences: your theme, language and cookie choice, in your browser only; never sent to us.

In this prototype the forms store nothing.

How collected, on what basis

Data are collected electronically when you fill in the contact form. The legal basis is the fulfilment of your request (article 5/2-c of Law 6698; article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Transfers

Data are not transferred to third parties in Türkiye or abroad.

Retention

Contact messages for at most one year after they are answered.

Your rights

Under article 11 of Law 6698 you may learn whether your data are processed, ask for correction or erasure, and object. Applications may be made in writing to the address above or to ipc@sabanciuniv.edu.
