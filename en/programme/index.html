Geopolitical Risk Academy

Macro Risk Academy measures global macro risk with data, explains it with a theoretical framework, and teaches it to decision-makers.

US–China rivalry, wars, rising economic protectionism and the crisis of the international order: in an age of multiple crises, global politics is growing less predictable. The noise is rising and the signal is harder to tell apart. Sound analysis under uncertainty is now a core competence.

The academy brings three things together: open data dashboards that measure macro risk, the theoretical framework that makes the data readable, and the training programme that turns both into practice. The dashboards are open to everyone; the programme runs in separate formats for individuals and organisations.

Training programme for individuals

Understanding and analysing geopolitical risks and developing strategies under uncertainty have become a fundamental qualification for individuals.

Designed for individuals across all professions, this programme bridges theory and practice in geopolitical risk analysis. It offers comprehensive training through simulations, case studies and data visualisation techniques.

Training programme for institutions and companies

This is an interactive training programme developed for mid- and senior-level corporate managers, company experts and analysts, and other economic decision-makers.

Focusing on how to integrate geopolitical developments into corporate decision-making processes, these modules offer companies ways not only to manage risks but also to turn global uncertainties into strategic opportunities. It includes an advanced, holistic training programme covering three stages of geopolitical and political risk analysis.

  • 10 interactive seminars
  • 1 simulation
