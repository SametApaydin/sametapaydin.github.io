Who it is for

Mid and senior managers, specialists and analysts in strategy, business development, corporate risk, internal audit, compliance, legal, finance, procurement, supply chain, foreign trade and investment teams. Participants from professional chambers, public institutions and local governments.

Model

A two-stage training model. The first stage covers analytical frameworks, core concepts and current global trends; the second, scenario development, case analyses, simulations, data visualisation and decision exercises. The first session of each day is theoretical, the second interactive.
