Who we are

Macro Risk Academy is a programme of the Istanbul Policy Center at Sabancı University.

The Istanbul Policy Center (IPC) is an independent policy research centre at Sabancı University. Macro Risk Academy grew out of IPC’s trade risk index work: first the data, then the dashboards, now the training.
